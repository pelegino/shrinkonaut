Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Shrinkonaut Studio is a private publishing tool used only by the owner of the Shrinkonaut YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

With the channel owner's permission through Google sign-in, it uploads videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and reads that channel's video and channel statistics (YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API).

What it stores

The sign-in token and the statistics are stored only on the channel owner's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No data about viewers or other Google users is collected.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
